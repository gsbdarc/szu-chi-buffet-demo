# Buffet browser demo

[Play the buffet](https://gsbdarc.github.io/szu-chi-buffet-demo/)

A desktop browser preview of the buffet study prototype. Open the link in current Chrome; Unity is not required.

Click **Start choosing**. **Look around / L** enables mouse-look, **Esc** restores the cursor, and **R** centers the view. Click a distant food to visit; click again after arrival to serve. **A / D** or **Previous / Next** moves along the counter. **Undo last** returns a serving. **Finish** confirms the plate.

Use **Export JSON** to download your choices. The demo does not upload choices to a study server. **Restart** starts a fresh plate. Phones/tablets and survey embedding have not been tested.
